The **Insights** tab summarises any period: how much you worked, when you usually start and finish, and where the hours went.

<Screenshot name="insights-year" alt="Insights for the last year: 1896 hours total, averages and hours by weekday" />

## Choosing a period

Pick **Last week**, **Last month** or **Last year**, a calendar **Month** or **Year**, or a **Custom** range. The dates in use are shown under the buttons.

<Screenshot name="insights-month" alt="Insights for last month" />

## Headline figures

| Figure | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Total worked | All booked time in the period |
| Avg / day | Average booked time on days with at least 2 hours |
| Max day | The longest day |
| Days worked | Days with at least 2 hours booked |
| Goal hit rate | Share of those days that reached your daily goal |
| Avg lunch | Average midday break between 10:00 and 14:00 |
| Discarded | Total gap time you chose to [discard](./tracking/gap-attribution.mdx) |
| Avg session | Average length of a continuous stretch on one task |

Alongside them are your earliest, average and latest start and finish times. Days with less than 2 hours booked are left out of the averages, so a quick weekend check-in doesn't skew them.

## Charts

Every chart uses your task colours.

- **Average hours by weekday**: which days are long and which are short.
- **Time by task** and **Hours per task**: where the time went, as a donut and as ranked bars.
- **Weekly hours**: week-by-week totals, which make holidays and crunch weeks easy to spot.
- **Hours by time of day**: when you actually work, hour by hour.
