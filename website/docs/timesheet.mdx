The **Timesheet** tab is your week as a grid: tasks down the side, Monday to Sunday across the top, and totals for every row and column.

<Screenshot name="timesheet-week" alt="Weekly timesheet for week 41 with a total of 39:15" />

## Reading the grid

- Rows are every task with time that week, including archived ones.
- Cells are `HH:MM`. A dash means no time.
- A small dot in a cell means some of that time has notes. Hover the cell to read them.
- Discarded gaps never appear.

## Choosing a week

Use **Previous** and **This week**, or type a week number and year. Weeks always start on Monday.

## Getting it out

- **Copy** puts the table on your clipboard, ready to paste into a spreadsheet or your company's timesheet tool.
- **CSV** saves the same table as a file.

Both use `HH:MM`, the same format as the grid.

## Week timeline

Below the grid, each day of the week is drawn on the same 24-hour scale, coloured by task. It makes the shape of the week obvious: early starts, long lunches, a short Friday.
