---
title: Snooze
---

Snooze keeps the clock running on the current task without idle prompts. Use it for meetings, workshops or reading on paper: anything where you're working but not touching the keyboard.

## Start a snooze

Right-click the tracker bar, choose **Snooze**, then pick:

- **1 hour**, **4 hours** or **8 hours**,
- **Until 17:00**,
- **Custom…** to choose a duration or an end time.

<Screenshot name="snooze-picker" alt="Snooze picker with Duration and End time tabs" width={360} caption="The custom picker. Enter snoozes, Esc cancels." />

You can only snooze while tracking. While a snooze is active, the bar shows a small `zᴢ` after the task name, and hovering it shows when the snooze ends.

## End a snooze

- **Early:** right-click the bar and choose **Exit snooze**. Normal idle detection starts again from that moment.
- **On time:** the snooze ends by itself. If you're still away when it ends, the next gap starts at the snooze end time, so the snoozed period stays on your task.

A snooze survives restarts. If TimeTrove is restarted during a snooze, it carries on tracking. If the snooze ran out while the app was closed, you'll be asked about the time after it ended.
