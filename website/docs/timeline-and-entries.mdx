title: Timeline and entries
---

The **Time entries** tab shows one day at a time: a total, a per-task breakdown, a 24-hour timeline and a table of every time slice. Use it to fix anything that didn't go to plan.

<Screenshot name="entries" alt="Time entries for Friday with the day timeline and entries table" />

## Choosing a day

Use **Previous** and **Next**, pick a date, or click **Today**. Under the date you'll see the day's total, followed by each task's share of it.

## The day timeline

Each coloured block is a time slice. Hover a block to see the task, its duration and any note.

- **Resize** a block by dragging its left or right edge. You can also focus an edge with Tab and use the arrow keys.
- **Add time** by dragging across an empty stretch (or clicking it). Choose a task in the bar that appears, then click **Add**.
- **Zoom** with **−** and **+**, or click **Fit** to zoom to the hours you actually worked.

The running slice can't be resized until it's stopped.

## The entries table

Each row is one slice, with its task, start, end and duration. The actions:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Note | Add, edit or clear a short note for that slice |
| Move | Reassign the slice to another task |
| Split | Cut the slice in two at a time you choose (the midpoint by default) |
| Delete | Remove the slice after you confirm |

Move and Split are available once a slice has ended.

## Adding an entry by hand

Click **Add entry**, pick a task, set the start and end, then click **Add**. The duration is shown as you type. Entries can't overlap other entries.

## Merge adjacent

Switching away and straight back can leave a row of short slices on the same task. **Merge adjacent** joins finished slices on the same task that are less than a minute apart, so the table stays readable.

## Notes

Notes are short and belong to one slice, for example "Export job retries" or "Pairing with Sam". Add them here, or from the switcher's **Add note** tab while you're working. Cells on the [timesheet](./timesheet.mdx) with notes show a small dot, and the notes appear on hover.
