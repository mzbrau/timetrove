If your Copilot plan has a monthly allowance of premium requests, TimeTrove can show how much you've used and whether you're on pace to run out before it resets.

<Screenshot name="copilot-usage" alt="Copilot tab showing 37% used, 110 of 300 requests, and 21 points ahead of pace" />

## Set up

1. In **Settings → GitHub**, tick **Enable Copilot usage tracking**.
2. Set **GitHub host** to `github.com` or your Enterprise Cloud host.
3. Paste an **API token**: a fine-grained personal access token with the **Copilot Requests** permission, or an OAuth or `gh` CLI token.
4. Set the **Copilot poll interval**. The default is 30 minutes, and it can be anything from 5 to 120.
5. Click **Test Copilot connection**.

TimeTrove reads the same per-user quota that VS Code shows. It never changes anything on GitHub.

### Monthly limit override

Your limit normally comes from GitHub. If GitHub doesn't report a usable limit (or reports it as unlimited), enter your allowance in **Monthly limit override**. Leave it empty to trust GitHub.

## On the tracker bar

When tracking is on, a percentage bubble appears to the right of the clock. It turns amber at 75% and red at 90%. Hover it for details, or click it to open the main window.

<Screenshot name="tracker-copilot" alt="Tracker bar with a Copilot bubble showing 37%" width={390} variant="bar" />

## The Copilot tab

- **Headline figures**: percentage used, requests used and remaining, the reset date, your plan and any overage.
- **Month pace**: whether you're ahead of or behind an even spread across the month. "21pp ahead" means you've used 21 percentage points less than an even pace would by today.
- **Quota pools**: premium requests, chat and completions, each with its own limit or "Unlimited".
- **Pace this month**: your actual usage plotted against the even-pace line.
- **Usage over time**: requests used and percentage used from every poll, with drops at each monthly reset.

Click **Refresh now** to poll immediately.
